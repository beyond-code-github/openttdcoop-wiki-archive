    3-way BBHs
    Back to junctionary
    Back to BBHs

    Three-way BackBone Hubs are the most common form of hubs after SLHs in general. The most interesting part of a BBH is usually the merger, which gets quite interesting even from the lowest line counts of LL_RR, up to lines counting more than LLL_RRR representing some true madness.

    The menu on the right side allows you to choose from 3-way hubs by the throughput of their strongest end, or browsing all of them together.
