    dih says bye

    1 April 2008, by dihedral

    As most of you may have picked up on – i never was a very active ‘player’. Probably always have and will be more of the ‘administrator’ type of person. Really – most of you are a fun bunch of people, but:In a lot of cases – some members and a few usual suspects (and wanna-be members -> gamer?) get on my nerves!
    Leaving me no other option than leaving this community!

    Should perhaps add some details:
    the main thing that got me Fussy was/is the internal communication (or lack there of), and often enough the
    unneeded arguments with new faces. On this note i would also like to say ‘sorry’ for not implementing the banner system in openttdlib. Oh – and i also appologize for the missing wwottdgd2, it would have been a lot of fun.
    thanks to all who helped enthusiasticlly.

    Last but not least: i had a good time here. i will leave the services i provide (bouncer, ts, svn) run for the time being – though at
    some point i might turn that of – yet not without prior notice… i aint a meany 😛

    enjoy and god bless
    dih
