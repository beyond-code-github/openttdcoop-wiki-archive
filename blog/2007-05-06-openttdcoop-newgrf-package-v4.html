#openttdcoop – NewGRF package v4

6 May 2007, by Osai

Today we released the final version of our NewGRF package version 4.
It comes with a load of changes:

  • Copyrights/Licenses for all NewGrfs (Information
  • Folder Structure (you need at least OpenTTD 0.5.1 or a recent nightly)
  • Updated Versions of the famous Total Town Replacement Set, Japan Set and much more
  • the folder ottdc_grfpack/old contains some grfs to make this pack compatible with “old servers”

Downloads:

  • Zip: Download (8.9 MB)

You can find more Information on our Wiki-Page and enjoy gaming with our all new package.
