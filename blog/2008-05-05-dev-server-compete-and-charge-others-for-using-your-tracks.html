    Dev-Server: compete and charge others for using your tracks!

    5 May 2008, by pm

    Today we started a new game on the openttdcoop.dev server. The patch tested is the track sharing patch currently under development by Gendemon (Forum thread).
    UPDATE 2: we now use the updated version 0.2 with an additional “move player” possibility to allow easier swaping of companies. For downloads see below.

    Please feel free to join, open your company or join an existing one (don’t use individual company pw here. Server has maybe pw ‘coop’ or no pw) and extensively test this feature. Besides being a really nice feature for the usual multiplayer games, giving them a completely new dimension, this patch is, if tested successfully, also a nice, big step forward to maybe a next incarnation of the next wwottdgd (World wide OTTD gaming day) with much more than just 10 clients connected - a real co-opetition event :).

    So far available: r12985IS.diff, linux binary (32bit), OS-X (intel) binary and Win binary (exe) or this (zip) thanks to Rob.

    3. Rob May 6, 2008 2:41 am

      This is a lot of fun. I really enjoy this style, where you have both a private network of your own, and the shared experience of creating a larger multi-company network. There are, however, a couple things that I think are really needed to make this workable. First, it should not be so easy or quick for people to make changes to their fees for using their shared resources. For example, they could easily start out by making their fees low, wait until their network is heavily utilized, then crank up the fees very high. If you weren’t constantly watching, which is very inconvenient, you could rapidly be bankrupt by this bait-and-switch. This is especially true of airports where the fees can be extremely high, and you can make frequent landing in rapid succession. This leads into my second point: There needs to be better reporting as to where your expenses are going. While knowing that your shared resource expenses went from 100,000 to 799,000 in one year is good, it doesn’t help when you can’t tell where that money is going. You’d need to go through the sharing specs for each other company and try to figure out if they changed, or if you changed something, etc. You should be able to see how much you paid to each other company for each shared resource (airport fees, track fees, depot fees, etc.)
