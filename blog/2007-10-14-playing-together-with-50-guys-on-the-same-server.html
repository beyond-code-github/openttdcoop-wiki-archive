    Playing together with 50 guys on the same server

    14 October 2007, by Ammler

    Wouldn’t that be a cool thing to try?

    [Screen from servers.openttd.org]

    dihedral and me talked about that and we came to the idea to make a “World Wide OpenTTD Game Day”. So, we are searching for a day where most people are online and we all should try to play together.

    But first we like to see, if that is technical possible, so we ask you to join the beta server with as many clients as you can. Here is a little howto for joining:

    1. Download your nightly here
    2. Downloadd our GRF Pack
    3. you should be ready to join

    Related links:

    • http://openttd.dihedral.de/
    • http://www.tt-forums.net/viewtopic.php?f=29&t=34429
    • http://www.openttd.org/server_detail.php?id=7318
    • irc://irc.oftc.net/wwottdgd

    Please join and enjoy playing with many-many others. 🙂
