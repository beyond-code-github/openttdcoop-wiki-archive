    Videos and Video Plugin updated

    19 July 2008, by Osai

    I finally updated all the movies and changed the format from flv to mpeg4. The new flash video player supports this format too and its easier to handle for me (encoding etc.) and more popular than flv. Besides I implemented the Simple Flash Video Plugin 1.5 which combines shadowbox and JW FLV MEDIA PLAYER 4.0.

    The current setup will allow a much easier and more functional implementation of videos. I hope, I can publish more interesting videos soon. If you experience any issues with the new video plugin report it at the IRC channel or send an email.

    You can find all our video posts in the Video Category
