    New member: Sylf

    9 April 2011, by V453000

    Yet again, an another person has been found worthy for our inner circles. Everybody say hello to our new member, Sylf!

    It has been quite some time since psg 192 where this fellow joined us the first time. From the very beginnings he always was very helpful and super productive. Learning with lightspeed, he quickly grasped all of the coop building. He always showed his friendliness and will to turn our games truly alive. Not only learning, but also buiding a whole lot of things and lasting in games through their whole length, not letting them to die out. Oh, and be careful: he likes madness!

    Congratulatons to Sylf and welcome to member club!
