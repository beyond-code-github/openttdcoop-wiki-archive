No coop, still awesome!

10 September 2007, by XeryusTC

It is not #openttdcoop related but I will still write an article about it: I got dreadlocks. And they are awesome :D.

It all started a few years ago when I wanted to have dreads but I didn’t have long enough hair to put them in. Finally I got long enough hair but was too lazy to start looking for someone who could put dreads in my hair. About two months ago I found out that a girl I know since ages could do it and so we eventually made an appointment. Unfortunately she couldn’t make it to that appointment because she was on vacation.

After another while we made another appointment and this time she did have time (and brought a friend)  and they started doing my hair. Which was about 5 hours of immensive pain and suffering, and the little saddists liked that.

But 5 hours later my dreads looked like this, now they’re about the same but a bit more fluffy (loose hairs mostly) because I slept without covering and stuff. There’s also one end that got a bit loose and it looks a bit out of place, but for everything else it is still about the same.

Now all of you also know what I look like :s.
