The New Mainserver Game - Next Generation

5 January 2007, by Osai

Do you remember our Werkloze Desyncs, Inc. Game on the Mainserver, based on the Big River Scenario?
Today we started a new game, with a similar plan, but even more impressive.
I call this type of game coop_NG, because the rules expand those of the Big River Game.
Here a short overview (more Details and an exact formulation will follow soon at our Wiki)

  • very low terraforming
  • eyecandy
  • two types of network:
    1. an express network for passengers and valuable goods on the base of shinkansen tracks -> long tunnels and long signalblocks, quite straight
    2. a normal network for passengers and goods -> short tunnels, curvy, short signalblocks
  • we will have Passerngers (with SBAHN) and Cargo Trains, well balanced
  • some ships, planes, trucks, factories and so on are good for the eyecandy
  • we will have trains only, no MagLev!
  • … I probably missed something

A hope we have some good screenshots of this game soon. Because it sounds really amazing.
Stay Tuned.
