Patched & promoted to membership

27 May 2008, by planetmaker

[image unavailable]
One of the first things I noticed of him was that in IRC he is one of the most smiling persons. And very helpful to noobs and newbies with big patience. Being involved in the OpenTTD dev team he started to lurk around our premises, having an eye for good and proper building and also patching ingame errors in the network - if time allows besides his quickly fixing a bug or writing another additions to OpenTTD. You may now already guess whom I’m speaking of.

So we happily and joyfully announce that we welcome SmatZ to our #openttdcoop team. A very warm welcome!
