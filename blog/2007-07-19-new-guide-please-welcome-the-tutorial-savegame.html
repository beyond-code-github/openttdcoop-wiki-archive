New guide – please welcome the “tutorial savegame”

19 July 2007, by Mucht

Hey Folks,

last night I completed the “tutorial savegame” guide. This guide is definitely ment for players totally new to #openttdcoop. It shows some very basic tricks of the trade – and the best thing: you can have it in full motion! Just download the savegame and see how things work out in the “real virtual world”. Along with that, you have some comments on the dedicated guide pages.

I appreciate some helping hands on both linguistic corrections and content-expanding commits on this section as well as on the whole #openttdcoop wiki 😉
