    Newgrf update

    10 March 2007, by XeryusTC

    Yes, another newgrf update, but this time I have a more important message than usual: I have updated some of Aegir’s newgrfs. This might break compatibility with old savegames, if it does you should download this old package.
    I also added some new grfs, also made by Aegir IIRC, so I hope you guys have fun with the new newstations.

    With this update also comes a restart of the sandbox, and guess what, we have a toyland game now so have some fun with the choo-choos.
