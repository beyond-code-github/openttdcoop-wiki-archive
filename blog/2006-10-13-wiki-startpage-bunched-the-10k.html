    Wiki-Startpage bunched the 10k

    13 October 2006, by Osai

    Somewhen these days the Wiki startpage reached the mark of 10.000 posts. I am blogging this, because the reaching of 1.000 has been promoted too. The wiki has been set up at 29th October 2005 and on 6 January 2006 (Picture) we reached the 1.000. As you can see on the picture, a lot of things have changed. And in my opinion we did a great job so far! Also I recognised the activity at our wiki is very low these weeks and a lot of things are not done yet. Maybe we try another update of the wiki, but somehow we were not able to do it, as thousands of php errors occured.

    Cheers on the next 10k hits at our startpage :p
