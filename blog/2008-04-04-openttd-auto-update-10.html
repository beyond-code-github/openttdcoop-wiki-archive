OpenTTD Auto Update 1.0

4 April 2008, by Mucht

Hello there,

today I stumbled upon a nice program. It’s written by Zuu and called OpenTTD Auto Update. Unlike other Updaters around Openttd, it has an interesting feature: it updates to the current revision used on our servers!

[The Main Window]
I’m not much of a technical nerd but I’m experienced enough to notice that this program makes extensive use of some sort of magic: it always knows which revision is currently used on our servers!

Just grab it here .

Finally, I want to admit that this blogpost is dedicated to a declining, small and cute community of MS-Windows users at #openttdcoop. See you at the servers!
