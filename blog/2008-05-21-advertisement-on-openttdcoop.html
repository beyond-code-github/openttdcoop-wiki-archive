Advertisement on #openttdcoop

21 May 2008, by Osai

Hello Community,

We decided to add some advertisement to our website. Right now this’ll only affect the blog, because our wiki is not such a good place for doing this. We all know ads are annoying and nothing good. But in general the purpose of these ads is not to gain any money. Instead we want provide the services, pay some bills (like the domain) and give you back the rest. The gained money will only be used for our community. Google AdSense is quite a good service and depending on the advertisers we can earn a lot: i.e. two days ago a single click was worth 0,90$. That’s amazing.

The members are still discussing this topic, but I want to encourage you to check the ads.
Maybe I’ll add another option, i.e users, being logged in, only see smaller ads at a different location.

Please give some feedback and check the running poll.
