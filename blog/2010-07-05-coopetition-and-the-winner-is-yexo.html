    Coopetition – and the Winner is: Yexo

    5 July 2010, by Mucht

    Hi there,

    a long time since I blogged the last time, huh? Well, as I always pointed out, I’ll always stay in touch with ‘coop – and so I accidentally ran into a coopetition challenge at Sunday 4th!

    As many of you may know, there is a cool mod out there named “Head to Head” for some reason: it features a map-generation logic which duplicates a landscape in order to offer a fair head2head competition gameplay! The mod is created and maintained by Yexo. Further information is to be found at the TT-Forums, the binary itself may be obtained at openttd.org.

    Well, the game itself turned out to be a disaster to our #openttdcoop members! As Ammler went bankrupt at the very beginning of the session, planetmaker decided to keep the gameplay on a very slow pace and I made several wrong decisions in the first game year, thus giving Yexo a good opportunity to outperform all of us! Eventually, we did not even find a plausibly reason to disqualify him so we had to admit his total victory over planet ‘coop – see the screenshot at the bottom for further details 😉

    Finally, I want to thank Yexo (although he degraded us ingame) for this great mod and I have to add that I will definitely be around for a new coopetition session in the next days or weeks!
