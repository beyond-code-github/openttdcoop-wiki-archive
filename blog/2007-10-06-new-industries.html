New Industries!

6 October 2007, by Ammler

The last point on the Roadmap to 0.6 seems to be done:

r11204 | belugas | 2007-10-05 02:21:40 +0000 (Fri, 05 Oct 2007) | 5 lines

-Feature: [NewGRF] Add support for Action 00 for Industries and IndustryTiles.
Thanks to :Csaboka for original concept, specs & help. Dalestan for technical explanations.
Pikkabird & Georges for detailed comments on their grf works. The Alpha testers (You know who you are).
And finally (but not least) Maedhros (newhouses), peter1138(newgrf) Rubidium and glx (numerous and welcome patches)
Remember: it certainly can be buggy. But be sure that behavior has been tested in TTDP (if you can) before raising a flag.

[meaworth-transport-28th-jul-1957.png]

It’s really awesome, what Belugas with his friends have done here. If yo like to try and discuss, we are currently playing it on our “Dev-Server” #openttdcoop.dev. Just join us on IRC and enjoy the new Industries:

[meaworth-transport-27th-nov-1957.png]

Currently we are playing with Pikkabird’s Basic Industries. I know of 2 other sets: Extended cargo schema and NewCargo, the last one seems to be good with Alpine (snowy temperate replacement for arctic), which is also working now.
