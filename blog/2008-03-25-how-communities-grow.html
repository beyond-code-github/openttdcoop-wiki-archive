    How Communities Grow

    25 March 2008, by dihedral

    [image unavailable] The first time i met him was on my “openttd.dihedral.de / FairPlay” games, and after a while playing there we asked him if he wanted to join the little admin-team - which he gladly did.
    always up for some interesting constructs. hardly to be seen on his own - mostly seeking to play with other people.

    On #openttdcoop public-server games his plans have been followed quite often. He is also very good at finding the root of jams and unclotting them. he builds in any area of the game may it be junctions, stations, mainlines, or whatnot.
    His last usual-suspect action is the Varible Trainlength Handeling, and by now you should know who i am talking about [:-)]

    Today we proudly announce Mark as new member of #openttdcoop and are glad he accepted. We are looking forward a lot of fun and and mz games with our newest member. As all #openttdcoop members, Mark will also be keeping an eye on the irc channel (op) and has full admin-access to the games.
