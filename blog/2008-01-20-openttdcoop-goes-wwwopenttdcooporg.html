#openttdcoop goes www.openttdcoop.org

20 January 2008, by Osai

Today we did another great step. We finally have our own top level domain www.openttdcoop.org. Our host Brianetta, who helped us doing this step, and the server ppcis.org stay the same.
I also want to thank TrueBrain for providing the nameserver.
All the members of #openttdcoop are provided with <name>@openttdcoop.org mail addresses.

Enjoy and keep on browsing!

p.s.: if you are using an RSS-Reader, you might update your rss feeds to reduce redirecting.

p.p.s.: (by dih) TB asked to credit his company, ISeeR, rather than him. For this we have added a link to our Blogroll, rather than just mentioning ISeeR in one little post [:-)]
