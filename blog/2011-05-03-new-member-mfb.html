New Member: mfb

3 May 2011, by Sylf

The openttdcoop team would like to welcome our newest member, mfb.

mfb joined the coop realm fairy recently, starting in PSG 199.  From the first days of playing in the PSG, he has made good contributions to the games, both in building skills and building atmospheres.  I’ve also seen that he is not always happy with traditions and conventions.  He’s always seeking for new designs without wrecking havoc. (I should have know, when I first saw his crazy new NOT gate…)

mfb, it’s been a pleasure to play with you, and I wish this trend will continue.  Welcome to the team!
