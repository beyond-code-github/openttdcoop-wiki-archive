    Servers at latest Nightly

    28 December 2006, by Mucht

    Tonight, we updated both Mainserver and Sandbox Server to OpenTTD r7603. This gives us the latest features of “bridges over a lot of stuff” and traction of locos.

    As you may have noticed, the status bar to the right seems not to work with this revisions anymore. We will try to fix this the next days!
