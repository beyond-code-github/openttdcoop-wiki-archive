Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year

23 December 2007, by Osai

[Merry Christmas]Hello everybody,
we wish you a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year. Enjoy your holidays and of course we hope all the wishes you have will be fulfilled.


I am really looking forward to the new year. A lot of interesting events and games are waiting for us.

  • The first #osqc will start at 1st January of 2008.
  • In the middle/end of January 2008 another World Wide OpenTTD Game Day is planned
  • More Public-Server Games
  • An empty ToDo-List

Update:
Due to request our servers will be closed down on 24th and 25th December! You should spend the time with your families. This’ll force you a bit.

Update 2:
The servers are online again. Enjoy gaming.

Cheers Coopers
