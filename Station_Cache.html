    Station Cache

    Idea: [fR4g]
    Wiki page: AntB

    [edit] Idea

    As some stations tend to get quite full quite quickly and later in the game there is little room for expansion, [fR4g] came up with Station Caches/Buffers.

    [edit] In Practice

    In practice, its ugly, messes with signal logic but does do the job with small stations preventing lock-ups on the line, virtually useless on larger stations however.
