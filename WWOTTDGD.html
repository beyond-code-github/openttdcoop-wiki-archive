    WWOTTDGD

    World Wide OpenTTD Game Day

    The world wide OpenTTD game day (WWOTTDGD for short) is based on a patch idea of Ammler, coming straight out of the needs of #openttdcoop: the community hit the hard-coded upper bound of 10 clients many times. Therefore, Ammler created [this patch].

    By now, the term stands for a compilation of patches in combination of #openttdcoop's GRF-Set. The project is mainly powered by dihedral, Ammler and Phazorx. Further details are available at the dedicated page on the OpenTTD wiki.
