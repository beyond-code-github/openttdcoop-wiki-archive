    Munkanélküli Desyncs
    Game Date: 08.04.07 - 31.10.07
    Usual supects: Ammler, thgergo, XeryusTC, Mucht, Kommer, hylje and Osai
    Gametype: Temperate - Rail - Cargo and SBahn-Concept
    Trainlength: 8-Tile trains
    Mapsize: 512x512 based on Water and Mountains Scenario
    Version used: Nightly r11361 + #openttdcoop-GRF-Set
    Remarks: This game is also know as Werkloze Desyncs 2 because we tried the same way of playing again. And it worked out. Due to the lack of time it took several month to complete this game. A lot of nice idea's had been created, like the well-known Osai Terminus Station, Cargo and Passenger Hubs in a special network, able to handle both, Cargo and Passenger trains, in one network and much more interesting stuff.
    The mainline in this area built by thgergo is merged into the city areas.
